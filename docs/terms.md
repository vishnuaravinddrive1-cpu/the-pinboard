## 3. What you can post

3.1 You may post a poster for a genuine, lawful event, with one poster per event. The event must:
- take place in Chennai or the area around Chennai (we decide what counts as "around Chennai", and may decline an event that is too far away); and
- be an experience, arts, knowledge, discussion or social event. For example: performances, exhibitions, workshops, talks, book and film events, walks and tours, classes, meetups, community gatherings and cultural events.

3.1A **Political topics.** Events about politics, policy or public affairs are welcome if they are neutral and meant to share knowledge, for example a talk, a panel with balanced voices, a workshop, a book discussion or a civics session. We do not accept events whose main purpose is to campaign for or against a candidate, party or cause, to raise votes or funds for one, or to run a rally or protest. We decide whether an event meets this rule, and our decision is final.

3.1B We may refuse, queue or remove any poster that does not fit these categories, even if it follows every other rule in these Terms.

3.2 You confirm that:
- you own the poster, or you have permission from everyone who owns any part of it (design, photographs, logos, fonts, music or artwork);
- the event is real, and the details on the poster, the event date and the booking link are accurate;
- if you give a booking link (it is optional), it goes to the real booking or registration page for that event.

3.3 You must not post anything that:
- is unlawful, hateful, abusive, harassing, defamatory or threatening, or promotes violence or discrimination;
- is sexually explicit, or exploits or endangers children;
- is misleading, fraudulent or a scam, including fake events, fake tickets or links that ask for payment or personal data under false pretences;
- links to malware, phishing or other harmful websites;
- infringes anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy or other rights;
- promotes illegal goods or services, gambling, or anything otherwise restricted by law;
- is political advertising or campaigning for a candidate, party or cause, or is a partisan rally (neutral, knowledge-giving political events are allowed under section 3.1A);
- is spam or repeats the same event.

## 4. How the board works

4.1 **Free service.** Pin Board is free for organisers and viewers. TCQ does not charge for it and does not earn from your posters.

4.2 **Board capacity.** The board shows a maximum of 100 live posters at a time. If the board is full, your poster joins a queue and goes live automatically when space opens. We do not guarantee that your poster will go live, or when, and we may reorder the queue at our discretion.

4.3 **Display order.** Posters appear in a collage, with soonest events nearest the front. We do not guarantee any particular position, size or visibility.

4.4 **Automatic removal.** Your poster is removed from the public board at midnight (IST) at the end of the event date you enter. You are responsible for entering the correct date. If you want a poster to stay up longer, ask us before the event date and we may extend it.

4.5 **Archiving.** After removal, we keep the poster and its details in an archive for our records, for reporting, and to resolve disputes. It will not be shown publicly. See section 8 for what this means for your data.

4.6 **Reminders.** We will email you a reminder about one day before your event date, as well as emails about your account and your posters.

## Notes for you (delete before publishing)

0. **Your name and address are now public in these Terms.** If 66, Baroda Street is your home, check you are comfortable with that. A TCQ office, a PO box or a business address would keep your home private. Also confirm with a lawyer whether TCQ is a registered entity, because that decides whether the Terms should name TCQ or you personally as the operator.

1. **Political content (3.1A):** "neutral and knowledge-giving" will sometimes be a judgement call. Write yourself a short internal checklist (for example: balanced speakers, no call to vote or donate, no party branding) so decisions stay consistent.
2. **"Around Chennai" (3.1):** consider setting a rough limit (for example, the Chennai metropolitan area plus nearby towns) so you can apply it fairly.
3. **Section 6 gives you wide discretion.** That suits a free community board, but it also means you decide what is acceptable. Write a short internal guideline for yourself so takedowns are consistent.
4. **Event safety:** you may want a stronger line that the organiser is responsible for permits and crowd safety.
5. **Privacy Policy:** a separate, plain-language page is a good idea.
