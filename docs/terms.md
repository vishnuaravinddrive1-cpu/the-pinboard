## 4. How the board works
At most 100 posters are live at once. If the board is full, new posters join a first-in, first-out queue and go live when a space opens. A poster stays up until the end of its event date (India Standard Time). We may take down any poster at any time. Posters are archived, not deleted, and images are kept for 3 years after the event date, after which we remove the image files and anonymise your details on those records.
