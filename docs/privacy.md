# Privacy

Pin Board is a free service run by {{name}} for {{org}}. Contact: {{contact}}.

## Viewers
You do not need an account. We count how many times a poster is opened and how many times its booking link is clicked, as daily totals. We do not store who you are or your IP address. We do not use advertising cookies. Your browser may remember that you dismissed the first-visit hint.

## Organisers
We keep your name, email address, your posters and the time you accepted the Terms. Emails about your account and posters are sent through Resend. Sign-in is handled by Supabase (and Google, if you choose it).

## Retention
Poster images are kept for 3 years after the event date. After that, image files are deleted and organiser details on those records are anonymised.

## Your choices
Write to {{contact}} to ask for your data to be corrected or removed.
